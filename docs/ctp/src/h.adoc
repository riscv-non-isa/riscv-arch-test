
=== H Hypervisor

The Hypervisor Extension adds an H coverage file containing new Hypervisor instructions, and additional coverage files related to exceptions, interrupts, and virtual memory.

==== HSm and H Hypervisor CSRs and Instructions

HSm boots to M-mode and tests the hypervisor CSRs and instructions from M-mode.  It also holds the
mstatus.TVM tests of HS-mode and VS-mode, whose traps must be taken in M-mode because the HS-mode trap
handler accesses satp and hgatp.  H boots to HS-mode and tests hypervisor CSRs and instructions in
HS, VS, U and VU modes.

==== ExceptionsHSm and ExceptionsH Hypervisor Exceptions

ExceptionsHSm boots to M-mode with nothing delegated and checks that exceptions from VS and VU reach M-mode whatever
hedeleg holds, ecall and ebreak from every mode, sret in VS-mode with mstatus.TSR = 1, HLV/HSV misaligned and access
faults taken in M-mode, and mtval2/mtinst.  ExceptionsH boots to HS-mode, walks hedeleg for exceptions from VS and VU,
checks that exceptions from HS and U ignore hedeleg, ecalls into HS-mode and VS-mode, vstvec, the virtual-instruction
exceptions, HLV/HSV privilege and priority, and hstatus.GVA, htval and htinst.  The trap handlers check htinst and
mtinst on every recorded trap.  The misaligned cp_xtinst cases run only on harts that trap misaligned accesses.

==== ExceptionsHF and ExceptionsHV Hypervisor Floating-Point and Vector Exceptions

ExceptionsHF and ExceptionsHV boot to HS-mode.  In HS, VS, U and VU modes, with each combination of mstatus.FS/VS and
vsstatus.FS/VS, they access fcsr or vl and execute a floating-point or vector instruction that changes state, which raises an
illegal instruction when either field in effect is Off and otherwise sets both to Dirty.

==== InterruptsHSm, InterruptsH and InterruptsHGei Hypervisor Interrupts

InterruptsHSm boots to M-mode and checks mideleg, mie and mip with the hypervisor interrupts, mtinst on interrupts,
and that M-level, HS-level and VS-level interrupts pending together in VS-mode or VU-mode are taken in that order.
InterruptsH boots to HS-mode and raises VS-level interrupts through hvip, checking hideleg, hie, hip, vsie, vsip,
priorities and delivery to HS-mode and VS-mode.  It also checks that hideleg bit 13 is read-only zero without
Shlcofideleg and that vsip and vsie show no LCOFI while it is zero, and that VS-level interrupts through a vectored
vstvec enter at BASE + 4 x the S-level cause.  It also executes WFI in VS-mode and VU-mode with each mstatus.TW
and hstatus.VTW.  Where the specification lets WFI either complete or raise an exception, nothing is pending, and
the Sail options wfi_is_nop and max_time_to_wait select the implementation's outcome.  InterruptsHGei covers guest external interrupts: hgeip, hgeie,
hip.SGEIP, hstatus.VGEIN, the SGEI priority and mip.SGEIP from M-mode.  hgeip is read-only, so only the platform
can raise a guest external interrupt, through the RVMODEL_SET_GUEST_EXT_INT and RVMODEL_CLR_GUEST_EXT_INT
hooks, which a DUT with GEILEN > 0 must define.  With GEILEN = 0, InterruptsHGei has no testcases.  The Sail
configurations raise them through the simple interrupt generator, and the Spike, QEMU and Whisper configurations
through IMSIC guest interrupt files.

==== ZicntrHSm and ZicntrH Hypervisor Counters

ZicntrHSm reads every counter from M-mode while walking mcounteren, hcounteren and scounteren together, and reads
time with each htimedelta.  ZicntrH checks counter access from HS, VS, U and VU modes against
mcounteren, hcounteren and scounteren, and time with htimedelta in each mode.  ZicntrH exercises hcounteren for all
32 counters, so no separate ZihpmH suite is needed.

==== SmcntrpmfH Hypervisor Counter Privilege Mode Filtering

SmcntrpmfH checks that the VSINH and VUINH bits of mcyclecfg and minstretcfg stop cycle and instret in VS-mode
and VU-mode.  With every mode inhibited the counter must not change across a visit to VS-mode or VU-mode; with
only that mode counting, cycle must advance and instret must advance by exactly the instructions retired there.

==== PMPH Hypervisor Physical Memory Protection

PMPH boots to M-mode and checks PMP on accesses from VS and VU modes (including M-mode accesses with MPRV = 1 and
MPV = 1), on HLV, HLVX and HSV, and under two-stage translation on the final supervisor physical address, on the
VS-stage and G-stage page-table reads and Svadu A/D updates, on AMOs and LR, and on the priority of page and
guest-page faults over PMP access faults.

==== SvHSm and SvH Hypervisor Two-Stage Virtual Memory

SvH boots to HS-mode and runs guest code under two-stage translation: VS-stage and G-stage permissions, PTE formats
and page sizes, SUM and MXR, Svade/Svadu, Svpbmt and Svnapot in each stage, guest-page faults with their htval and
htinst (including implicit VS-stage page-table accesses and accesses that straddle a page), hedeleg, HLV/HLVX/HSV,
GPA widths, the vsatp, satp and hgatp MODE fields, and the guest fences.  SvHSm boots to M-mode for mstatus.MPRV
with MPV = 1 and guest traps taken in M-mode, including mtval2 for straddling accesses.  SvH also covers the
SvnapotH and SvaduH tabs.

==== SstcHSm and SstcH Hypervisor Timer Compare

SstcHSm checks from M-mode vstimecmp access, hip.VSTIP as hvip.VSTIP OR the vstimecmp comparison with each
menvcfg.STCE and henvcfg.STCE, htimedelta (including 64-bit wrap), and VS timer interrupts armed from M-mode.  SstcH
checks vstimecmp and stimecmp access from HS, VS, VU and U modes and VS timer interrupts armed from HS-mode.

==== SsstateenH and SmstateenH Hypervisor State Enable

SsstateenH walks hstateen0 from HS-mode and checks that hstateen0.SE0 and ENVCFG control sstateen0 and senvcfg
accesses from VS and VU modes but not from HS-mode.  With Smstateen it first enables only the mstateen0 bits it
controls and sets and clears bit 63 of mstateen1-3; without Smstateen it runs with hstateen alone.  SmstateenH checks that mstateen0.SE0, ENVCFG and P1P13 control accesses to hstateen0, sstateen0, henvcfg,
senvcfg and hedelegh from HS, VS and VU modes, and that hstateen0 bits whose mstateen0 bit is 0 are read-only zero.
The CSRIND, AIA, IMSIC and CTR bits of hstateen0 are not tested because Sail does not model the state they
control.

==== SvinvalHSm and SvinvalH Hypervisor Memory Management Table Invalidation

SvinvalH executes the Svinval instructions in HS, VS, U and VU modes with hstatus.VTVM clear and set, and checks that
the Svinval sequences remove changed VS-stage and G-stage leaves.  SvinvalHSm executes them in M-mode, and in the
lower modes with mstatus.TVM = 1, whose traps are taken in M-mode.

==== ZicfilpH Hypervisor Landing Pads

ZicfilpH boots to HS-mode.  It checks that henvcfg.LPE, not menvcfg.LPE, enables landing pads in VS-mode and
that senvcfg.LPE, not henvcfg.LPE, enables them in VU-mode.  It checks that traps into VS-mode save ELP in
vsstatus.SPELP and traps into HS-mode in sstatus.SPELP, and that SRET restores ELP from vsstatus.SPELP when
executed in VS-mode and from sstatus.SPELP when executed in HS-mode.

==== SdtrigH Hypervisor Debug Triggers

SdtrigH boots to HS-mode and checks that mcontrol6, icount, itrigger and etrigger triggers fire in VS-mode and VU-mode
only when their vs or vu bit is set, the H-only textra selects (mhselect against mcontext and hgatp.VMID, sselect = 2
against vsatp.ASID), hcontext as an alias of mcontext, and the control of scontext and hcontext by
mstateen0.CONTEXT and hstateen0.CONTEXT.  Its normative rules are listed with SdtrigSm.

==== SsnpmH Hypervisor Pointer Masking

SsnpmH boots to HS-mode and runs with vsatp = Bare and hgatp = Sv57x4 or Sv48x4, so pointer masking zero-extends
guest physical addresses, including their two bits beyond the VS-stage widths.  It checks that henvcfg.PMM masks
VS-mode accesses and senvcfg.PMM VU-mode accesses; that HLV and HSV take henvcfg.PMM with hstatus.SPVP = 1, and
senvcfg.PMM from HS-mode or hstatus.HUPMM from U-mode with SPVP = 0; and that HLVX, and accesses with
vsstatus.MXR = 1, are not masked.
