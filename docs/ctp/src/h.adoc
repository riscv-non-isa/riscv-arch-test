
=== H Hypervisor

The Hypervisor Extension adds an H coverage file containing new Hypervisor instructions, and additional coverage files related to exceptions, interrupts, endian, and virtual memory.

==== H Hypervisor CSRs and Instructions

The H suite has two groups of tests.  The H group boots to HS-mode and tests hypervisor CSRs and instructions in
HS, VS, U and VU modes; its covergroups are H_hscsr_cg, H_vscsr_cg, H_ucsr_cg and H_inst_cg.  The Sm group boots
to M-mode and tests the hypervisor CSRs and instructions from M-mode.  It also holds the mstatus.TVM tests of
HS-mode and VS-mode, whose traps must be taken in M-mode because the HS-mode trap handler accesses satp and hgatp;
its covergroups are H_Sm_mcsr_cg, H_Sm_tvm_cg and H_Sm_inst_cg.  The Sm group requires Sm, so it is not part of a
certificate without Sm, such as RVA23.
